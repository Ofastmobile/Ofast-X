Ofast Toolkit: The Ultimate Modular WordPress Plugin.
Ofast Toolkit is a powerful, modular WordPress admin toolkit that gives site owners, developers, designers, and agencies everything they need to manage, harden, brand, and supercharge their WordPress installation without juggling a dozen separate plugins.

With Ofast Toolkit, you get everything in one place:
A code snippet manager that keeps your custom PHP organized. 
A login redesign that makes your admin feel like yours. 
SMTP that actually delivers your emails. 
Spam protection that stops bots without annoying real users. 
Social login so your members don’t have to create another password. 
Bulk SMS to reach your customers directly. 
Security hardening that closes the doors brute-force attackers walk through every day. 
A fully redesigned admin interface with glassmorphism styling, light/dark mode, and a modern dashboard that makes logging in feel like opening a premium product.

And because every site is different, every module is independently toggleable. Activate only what you need. The rest stays out of your way.
