SHARED HOSTING EMAIL LIMITS (REFERENCE)
================================================================================

Provider        | Emails/Hour | Emails/Day | PHP Timeout
----------------|-------------|------------|------------
Bluehost        | 150         | 500        | 30s
SiteGround      | 100-500     | 500        | 30s
GoDaddy         | 250         | 500        | 30s
Hostinger       | 200         | 500        | 60s
Namecheap       | 50          | 500        | 30s
DreamHost       | 100         | 200        | 60s

WHAT HAPPENS WHEN EXCEEDED:
- Exceed hourly limit → Emails silently dropped
- Exceed daily limit → All emails blocked until next day
- PHP timeout (30s) → Script dies, remaining emails lost
- Too many at once → Flagged as spam, IP damaged
- Repeated abuse → Account suspended
