The 1.5:1 ratio is smart—it gives the form prominence while keeping the preview visible. The responsive breakpoint at 900px shows you're thinking about real-world usage.

The focus state with the soft glow is chef's kiss. This is how modern forms should feel—the 4px rgba shadow creates a beautiful depth without being distracting.
